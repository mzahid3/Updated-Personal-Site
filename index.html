Welcome to My Website

My name is Muhammad Naveed Zahid, born in Multan, Punjab, Pakistan. Although my full name starts with Muhammad, I go by Naveed. In Pakistan, it's common to use middle names; here in the U.S., people often call me Muhammad until I clarify it.

I completed high school in Pakistan through 10th grade and was in 11th grade when we moved. Since high school here goes up to 12th, I had to repeat a year. I graduated from Bolingbrook High School, then attended Joliet Junior College, and now study Computer Information Systems at Benedictine University.

My family started the immigration process when I was 3 or 4, and we moved in 2012. I spent 17 years in Pakistan and miss it deeply. I’ve only visited once since, but hope to visit more often after graduation.
